CLEAR BOUNDARIES

Privacy & MVP limits

Birth details and journal entries are processed in your browser. There is no account, cloud chat, analytics or payment service in this MVP. The hosting provider still receives normal page-request metadata.

Details are saved only when you choose to save them. Browser storage is not encrypted and can be read by someone with access to your browser profile. Erasing data here removes only this app's records.

Western planetary positions are calculated locally with Astronomy Engine. Houses, ascendant, full Vedic/Kundali analysis, paid reports and live LLM conversation are not available. Unknown birth times use a clearly labeled noon reference.

Astrology and tarot are not scientifically established predictions, diagnoses or financial/legal advice. Curated interpretations have limited topic coverage and have not been certified by a professional astrologer.

NOT AVAILABLE IN THIS MVP

Vedic / Kundali

The full Vedic engine, Lahiri conventions, divisional charts and dasha calculations are still under validation. This app will not relabel tropical positions as a Kundali. The Western planetary chart and tarot are available now.
