    CLEAR BOUNDARIES

    Privacy & limits

    Birth calculations, PDF generation and journal entries happen in your browser. Choosing Search sends only the place query to Photon by komoot, based on OpenStreetMap; the provider also receives normal connection metadata. The birth name, date and time are not sent. Manual coordinates avoid the search service. Map links open OpenStreetMap only when selected. Present-day place data and timezone suggestions may need historical correction.

    Your Vedic or Western preference is remembered in this browser, separately from birth details, which are saved only when you opt in. A second person's comparison profile requires permission and stays only in this tab; it is not saved or sent to AI. Tarot questions and selected cards stay in this tab unless you save the reflection to your local journal.

    Generated conversation requires separate consent to send your question, recent conversation and selected sign placements to Cloudflare Workers AI. Your name, raw birth date/time, birthplace and journal are excluded unless you type them into a question. The provider still processes connection metadata. Do not enter sensitive identifying details.

    There is no account, analytics or payment service. Saved browser data is not encrypted. Downloaded PDFs contain the birth details shown in the report. Erasure removes this app's saved records, preference, current tarot reading, comparison profile and in-tab AI consent; it cannot delete an already downloaded PDF or recall a request sent to a provider.

    Vedic mode uses approximate Lahiri, mean nodes, D1/D9, whole-sign houses and a traditional Vimshottari timeline. This is not every Kundali analysis. Unknown time withholds precise claims; lagna/houses need a supported location and time. Two-person comparison is unscored; Guna Milan, dosha matching and synastry interpretations are not calculated. Reviewed paid reports and palm-line analysis are not offered. The sample chart is never used for a personal answer.

    Astrology and tarot are not scientifically established predictions or professional advice. Free AI is rate-limited and may be unavailable; there is no paid or scripted personal fallback. Provider free allowances can change. PDF tables are searchable; complex-script personal labels may be local images and are not searchable. The PDF is not a certified accessible or professionally reviewed report.

    BEFORE AN AI CONVERSATION

    Your choice to share

    Your question, up to three recent exchanges, and selected planetary placements will be sent to Cloudflare Workers AI. Your name, raw birth details and journal stay here unless you type them into a question. Please avoid sensitive identifying information.

    Cloudflare states it does not use Workers AI customer content to train models without explicit consent. Our backend is designed to store quota counters, not conversations. Provider processing still occurs outside your device.

    The model may make mistakes. It cannot establish a reliable marriage date or another person's feelings. Only the free allowance is used; if unavailable, chat stops rather than charging or substituting a scripted reading.

    CALCULATION CONVENTIONS

    Vedic / Lahiri

    This view uses a date-dependent Lahiri approximation, mean Rahu/Ketu, whole-sign houses, nakshatra/pada, D1/D9 positions and a 365.25-day Vimshottari year. It does not claim every school or every Kundali analysis.

    Reference fixtures cover selected dates and locations; this is not exhaustive certification. Near sign, pada or divisional boundaries, small birth-time or calculation differences can change a result. Lagna/houses require known time and location and are unavailable at polar latitudes of 66 degrees or above.

    Unknown birth time is a noon reference only: D9, nakshatra/pada and dasha timing are withheld. These traditional calculations do not establish guaranteed life outcomes.
