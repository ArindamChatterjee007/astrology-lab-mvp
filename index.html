      CLEAR BOUNDARIES

      Privacy & limits

      Birth calculations, PDF generation and journal entries happen in your browser. Choosing Search sends only the place query to Photon by komoot, based on OpenStreetMap; the provider also receives normal connection metadata. The birth name, date and time are not sent. Manual coordinates avoid the search service. Map links open OpenStreetMap only when selected. Present-day place data and timezone suggestions may need historical correction.

      Your Vedic or Western preference is remembered in this browser, separately from birth details, which are saved only when you opt in. A second person's comparison profile requires permission and stays only in this tab; it is not saved or sent to AI. Tarot questions and selected cards stay in this tab unless you save the reflection to your local journal.

      Chat and hosted AI are not part of this release. Written previews are composed locally from calculated placements and original fixed interpretations. No report or birth profile is sent to an AI service.

      Palm requests require separate adult and photo consent. Selecting a photo prepares a smaller JPEG on your device and removes image metadata; nothing uploads until you confirm. Prepared photos, contact details and questions go to our private Cloudflare order service for manual review, not to an automatic AI model. Photos become inaccessible after 7 days and are removed by the hourly cleanup; you can delete them earlier using your receipt. Your written reading is available here after it is uploaded. No automatic email delivery is configured.

      Lal Kitab orders send your name, email, birth details and questions to our order service when you confirm the test payment, so your report can be prepared. Palm requests use the separate photo consent described above. Completed PDFs are stored in the private order service for download. Payment is currently a test gateway: no card details are collected and no money is charged. Order IDs and access codes you receive are kept in this browser for convenience.

      There is no account or analytics service. Saved device data is not encrypted by this app. Palm photos and question drafts are not saved to browser storage; only receipts and the click-sound preference are remembered. Exported PDFs contain the details shown in the report. Native export opens a system share sheet and keeps an app-cache copy until erasure or the next export session. Erasure clears local records and drafts; it cannot recall a sent request or delete external files. Use the palm request's Delete uploaded photos control for server copies.

      Vedic mode uses the Lahiri ayanamsa, mean Rahu/Ketu, D1/D9, whole-sign houses and the Vimshottari dasha. Lagna, houses, nakshatra and dasha dates need your exact birth time and place. Matchmaking scores Ashtakoota Guna Milan (36 points) and the Manglik check on this device from both people's Moon positions; the second person's details stay in this tab only. The sample chart and sample couple are never used for a personal reading.

      PDF tables are searchable; complex-script personal labels may be embedded as images.

      CALCULATION CONVENTIONS

      Vedic / Lahiri

      This chart uses the Lahiri ayanamsa, mean Rahu and Ketu, whole-sign houses, nakshatra and pada, D1 and D9 positions and a 365.25-day Vimshottari year.

      Near a sign, pada or divisional boundary, a few minutes of birth time can move a planet into the next division, so give your most accurate birth time. Lagna and houses need a known time and a location below 66 degrees latitude.

      Without a birth time the chart is cast for 12:00 noon; sign placements hold unless a planet changed sign that day, and lagna, nakshatra, D9 and dasha dates are added once the time is known.
