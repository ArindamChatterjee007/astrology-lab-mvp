CLEAR BOUNDARIES

Privacy & MVP limits

Birth calculations and journal entries stay in your browser. Generated conversation, when enabled, requires separate consent to send your question, recent conversation and selected chart placements to Cloudflare Workers AI. Your name, raw birth date/time and journal are not included in that payload. Do not put sensitive identifying details in your questions.

There is no account, analytics or payment service. The hosting provider still receives normal request metadata. Saved browser data is not encrypted. Erasure removes this app's saved records and resets its in-tab conversation consent; it cannot recall a request already sent to a provider.

Full Vedic/Kundali, houses, ascendant and paid reports are unavailable. Unknown birth times never supply precise placements to the companion. The sample chart is never used for a personal answer.

Astrology and tarot are not scientifically established predictions or professional advice. Generated chat has no paid fallback, and no personal answer is substituted if generation fails or is disabled. Provider free allowances are limited and can change.

BEFORE AN AI CONVERSATION

Your choice to share

Your question, up to three recent exchanges, and selected planetary placements will be sent to Cloudflare Workers AI. Your name, raw birth details and journal stay here unless you type them into a question. Please avoid sensitive identifying information.

Cloudflare states it does not use Workers AI customer content to train models without explicit consent. Our backend is designed to store quota counters, not conversations. Provider processing still occurs outside your device.

The model may make mistakes. It cannot establish a reliable marriage date or another person's feelings. Only the free allowance is used; if unavailable, chat stops rather than charging or substituting a scripted reading.

NOT AVAILABLE IN THIS MVP

Vedic / Kundali

The full Vedic engine, Lahiri conventions, divisional charts and dasha calculations are still under validation. This app will not relabel tropical positions as a Kundali. The Western planetary chart and tarot are available now.
